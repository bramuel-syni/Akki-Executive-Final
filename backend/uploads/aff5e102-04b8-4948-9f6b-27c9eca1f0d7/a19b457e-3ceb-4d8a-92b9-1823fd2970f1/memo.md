# Q3 Strategic Brief — Project Lighthouse

Author: CFO Office | Date: 2026-06-01 | Classification: Internal

## 1. Executive Summary

Project Lighthouse is our proposed market expansion into the East African region, specifically Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania. The initiative requires capital allocation of $18M over 24 months. We have material gaps in our regional regulatory understanding, especially around the proposed Kenya Data Protection Act amendments expected in Q4. The board must decide on go/no-go by July 15.

## 2. Open Strategic Questions

Several material questions remain unresolved that the board should consider before the July 15 vote:

- What is the true addressable market size for our SaaS product line across the three East African countries? Our consultant's report estimates $240M TAM but external benchmarks suggest $90M-$140M.
- Should we partner with Safaricom as a distribution channel, or build a direct-to-enterprise sales motion through Nairobi?
- How exposed are we to KES devaluation risk over the 24-month horizon, and do we need an FX hedge program?
- What is the regulatory pathway for our financial-services module under the new Central Bank of Kenya digital lending rules?
- Do we have the in-region talent to staff three country GMs within six months, or do we need an executive search firm engagement?

## 3. Capital Plan

Tranche 1 (Months 0-6): $4M for Nairobi office, legal entity setup, first 12 hires.
Tranche 2 (Months 7-18): $9M for Uganda + Tanzania expansion and product localization.
Tranche 3 (Months 19-24): $5M for marketing, channel build-out, and contingency.

## 4. Key Risks

Currency volatility, regulatory uncertainty in Kenya, talent acquisition cost inflation, and possible double-taxation exposure if our IP holding structure is not adapted before Q4. Our tax counsel is reviewing whether to migrate IP to the Mauritius holding company.

## 5. Recommendation

Conditional approval pending answers to the open strategic questions above. CFO recommends commissioning a regional market-sizing study from McKinsey or Dalberg before tranche 2 deploys.
