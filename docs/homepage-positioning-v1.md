# Homepage Positioning v1

This is the rules doc for AKKI's homepage. The next person who rewrites the homepage starts from here, not from scratch. Argue against this; do not ignore it.

## Buyer (single, abstract)
The people who sit at the top table — operating executives (CEO, CFO, CHRO, CTO, COO) and non-executive directors (Chairs, NEDs) at listed and pre-IPO companies. The hero treats them as one audience, role-light. Role-specific pages live below.

## Primary site goal
Convert a warm visitor (LinkedIn, referral, Exco360) into an early-access registration in under 90 seconds. Sandbox stays available as a live-demo path; it is the third door, not the first.

## CTA hierarchy (do not invert)
1. Primary: **Apply for early access** → /early-access
2. Secondary: **How AKKI thinks** → /about#methodology
3. Tertiary text link: **See it live in 60 seconds →** → /sandbox

## Editorial rules
- Voice: Financial Times leader column, not SaaS landing page. Short sentences. Concrete claims.
- Banned terms: supercharge, unlock, 10x, game-changing, revolutionise, AI-powered (as a standalone claim), intelligent (adjective), smart, "in today's…", "in an era of…", "more than ever".
- No exclamation marks anywhere in body copy.
- No metaphor headlines. Hero must name the pain literally.
- Numbers and citations beat adjectives.
- Cream / oxblood / navy palette only. Georgia serif heads. No new tokens.

## Structural rules
- 7 sections, in this order: Hero · 60-second proof · Sharpest use case · Trust · Voice · (no §6 — price was dropped at v1; bring it back when pricing is set) · Closing CTA.
- One primary action per section. If a section needs two buttons, it is wrong.
- Trust strip lives below the hero CTAs, no scroll required.
- The closing CTA copy is identical to the hero CTA. Same words, same destination.

## Off-homepage rules
- Three-pillar structure (Solve / Cross-Board Pulse / Studio) lives at /features, not /.
- Methodology lives as an anchor on /about (not a new page).
- Plans / Status / Terms / Privacy are deliberately hidden from footer at v1. Add when content exists.
- Enterprise has its own public page; do not link the protected /app/enterprise from marketing.

## Image rules
- Local, not hot-linked. Sepia, desaturated, editorial. No screens, no smiles, no logos in image.
- Generated via nano_banana, hosted at /static/marketing/.

## Validation
The homepage is correct when:
- A reader who lands cold can name the pain in 5 seconds.
- A listed-company director can find the audit trail and sensitivity claim above the fold scroll.
- A reader who scrolls completes either an early-access registration or a Sandbox session within 90 seconds of arrival.

## Rejected v1 headlines (do not re-propose without new evidence)
- "AKKI reads the pack so you can read the room." (metaphor — asks reader to translate)
- "Your next board meeting starts before you open the pack." (too long; lands flat without rhythm)
- "280 pages on Friday. Three questions on Tuesday." (sharp but loses the "prepared" payoff)

## Things that are NOT homepage real estate
- Feature catalogue — that's /features.
- Three pillars — that's /features.
- Pricing tables — when ready, that's /plans.
- Client logos — only if real and permissioned.
- Blog feed — Exco360 is voice, not navigation.
- NED-vs-Exec persona split — that's /about.
