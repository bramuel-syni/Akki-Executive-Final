# Early access

Akki is in early access. Here's what that means and how to request a
place.

## What it does

Early access is the period before Akki ships broadly. We admit a
small group of executives, NEDs, and committee-level operators to use
it first. We read every request personally and reply within three
business days.

## How to use it

1. Visit `/cohort` (or click "Request access" anywhere on the site).
2. Fill in the form — name, work email, your organisation, your
   role, the question you most want Akki to help with, and how you
   heard about us.
3. Submit. You'll see a confirmation on the page; we'll email a
   receipt to the address you used.
4. We reply within 3 business days. If your situation isn't a fit
   for what Akki currently does, we'll say so plainly.

**Worked example.** A NED on three boards drops a request. She lists
the question as "we keep landing on different reads of the same M&A
thesis and need a way to surface why." We reply two days later with
a 20-minute call invite to walk her through Akki on her actual
question, not a demo session.

## Common questions

- **Is there a fee?** Pricing is still being finalised. We will
  share the offer with you before launch, not during the request.
- **How long does early access last?** Until Akki ships broadly,
  which we currently target for 2026 Q3-Q4. Early access seats stay
  on early-access pricing for two years from sign-up.
- **What happens to my data if I leave?** Your sessions are yours.
  Export at any time; deletion is permanent and runs within 24 hours.

## Troubleshooting

- **The form errored out.** Refresh and try again. If it errors
  twice, email info@syni.ai with the same fields and we'll capture
  it manually.
- **I haven't heard back.** Three business days is the cap. If
  you've waited longer, reply to the confirmation email — it goes
  straight to a real person.
